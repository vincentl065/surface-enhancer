Which segment?

Drop your segment folder here (or a .zip of it) A segment is the folder that holds x.tif, y.tif, z.tif and meta.json. Only these files are read, and they stay on this computer.
Where is my segment?
  • You traced it in VC3D: it is a folder inside your volume package, <your scroll>.volpkg/paths/<segment id>/ (also traces/ or export/). Choose that folder; or open the whole package below, under “I use VC3D”.
  • You downloaded a published segment: on the Vesuvius Challenge data server it is the …/segments/<id>/mesh/…tifxyz/ folder of the scroll. Download that folder (four small files), then choose it here. Take the one placed on the scan you picked in step 1.
  • You only have an .obj or a .ppm: convert it to tifxyz with the VC3D tools first (vc_obj2tifxyz).

Volume to read the scan from

Local tool: the page talks only to the server on this computer. Every gesture is saved at once; the original surface is never modified.

How it works

  1. Add a segment. A segment is a tifxyz folder (the format of Volume Cartographer / VC3D). Give the scan volume it was traced on.
  2. Preload, once. The scan is read along the normal of the surface, 40 voxels above and below, and stored as a “straightened block” (about 81 bytes per pixel of the flat page). After that, nothing is read from the scan again: page, sections, 3D and scores are instant.
  3. Retouch. In the editor, the flat page is on the left and two sections of the scan on the right. Drag the orange line onto the sheet, or let the magnet do it. Scores show where to look. Undo is unlimited.
  4. Export. Download a zip (tifxyz + offset field + journal), or write a new segment into your VC3D volume package. The original is never modified.

Grids coarser than 8 voxels between nodes (VC3D traces are often at 20, whole-scroll sheets at 16) are resampled to 4 voxels for the retouch; the export can give back the original node layout.

Command line: python -m surface_enhancer --help. Documentation: the docs/ folder of the project.
